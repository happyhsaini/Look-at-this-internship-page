Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is internship remote?
Yes. The internship is virtual, so students can complete training tasks, project work, and submissions from home with a flexible schedule.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes. Students who complete the required tasks and final project receive an internship completion certificate with a unique completion ID.
What is the duration?
The available internship durations are 1 month and 2 months. Students can choose the option that fits their college schedule and learning goals.
Is placement support available?
Career support is included through resume tips, project portfolio guidance, interview preparation, and direction on how to apply for beginner-friendly roles.
Can I apply for multiple programs?
Yes, but it is better to complete one track first so your final project is focused and portfolio-ready.
Do I need prior experience?
Basic computer knowledge is enough for beginner tracks. The roadmap starts with fundamentals and moves toward project work step by step.
